📚 How It Works

  1. The organizer deploys the smart contract.
  2. The organizer manually starts a round.
  3. Students enter by contributing test ETH.
  4. Each student can enter only once per round.
  5. A student's contribution determines their probability of winning.
  6. The organizer manually stops the round.
  7. The smart contract selects a weighted winner.
  8. The winner's probability is calculated from their contribution relative to the total pot.
  9. The winner receives: min(contribution × (1 + probability), total pot)
  10. Any remaining ETH goes to the organizer.
  11. A new round is automatically prepared, but the organizer must manually start it.

Example

Suppose the total pot is 0.010 ETH and a player contributed 0.006 ETH.

Pᵢ = 0.006 / 0.010 = 0.60
Reward = 0.006 × (1 + 0.60) = 0.0096 ETH

Because 0.0096 ETH is below the 0.010 ETH pot, the player receives 0.0096 ETH.

If a player contributed 0.007 ETH to a 0.010 ETH pot:

Reward = 0.007 × (1 + 0.70) = 0.0119 ETH

The reward is capped at the pot:

Final Reward = min(0.0119, 0.010) = 0.010 ETH
